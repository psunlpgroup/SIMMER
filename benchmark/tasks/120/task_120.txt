Task: How to make Shrimp and Avocado Salad

--- Plan ---

Step 1: Ingredients
2 lbs of shrimp (15 ct per pound)
Extra Virgin Olive Oil
S&P
Mayonaise (I used olive oil mayo)
lime juice
2 avocados
dill (If you have fresh use it, it tastes better)
white wine or champagne vinegar
red onion

Step 2: Roast the Shrimp
Preheat your oven for 425F. Peel and wash shrimp. Pat dry and place on baking sheet.  Mix about 2 tbls of olive oil and about a tsp of salt and pepper and coat the shrimp evenly. Place shrimp in one layer on baking sheet, but don't over crowd.  I find that they tend to steam more, rather than roast when the pan is too crowded with shrimpies. Bake for about 10 min.

Step 3: The Sauce
While the scrimps are baking, you can whip up the sauce. In a bowl, mix about 3/4 cup of mayo, dill, a splash of white wine vinegar, lime juice (this also keeps the avocado from browning) and mix. Add chopped red onions.

Step 4: Shrimp Time!
Once the shrimp are cooked, pull out of the oven. Place warm shrimp into an empty bowl.  Add sauce to the shrimp.

Step 5: Avocado Time
Slice up your avocado and cube. Fold the avocado into the shrimp.  The warmth from the shrimp will help get all those great flavors to marry.  Let cool in the fridge for at least an hour, Once its cool, taste, and add salt if needed.
