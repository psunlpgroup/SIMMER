Task: How to Make Banana Bread Loaf

--- Plan ---

Step 1: Preheat oven to 350ºF/175ºC.
Lightly grease a loaf pan with butter or cooking spray.

Step 2: Combine the flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt in a large bowl.


Step 3: Mix 3 together the brown sugar and butter in a separate large bowl until you achieve a creamy texture.

Step 4: Stir the eggs and mashed bananas into the butter/sugar mixture until well blended.


Step 5: Add the banana mixture, vanilla extract, and banana flavoring into the flour mixture.
Stir just to moisten.

Step 6: Pour batter into loaf pan.
Remember to leave room in the pan for the bread to rise as it bakes.

Step 7: Bake in preheated oven for 60 to 65 minutes (depending on your oven type time may vary), or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the bread comes out clean.


Step 8: Let the bread cool in pan for 10 minutes, then invert onto a cooling rack or plate.


Step 9: Serve by itself or with any topping you like.
