Task: How to make mexican layer pie

--- Plan ---

Step 1: Brown and Season the Mince
Using a very small amount of oil, add the mince, the coriander, the cumin, the mustard powder, and the Chili powder to a large frying pan. Cook on a medium heat until the meat has browned.

Step 2: Dice the Vegetables
Dice the peppers and the onions. I used a dicer with a fine grid to do this. it is important that the vegetables have a uniform size. Add the diced vegetables to the browned meat and continue to cook until the vegetables have softened and the onion is translucent.

Step 3: Make the Sauce
Into the meat and the vegetable mixture, stir 2 tablespoons of tomato purée, One tablespoon of garlic, One tablespoon of soy sauce and season with salt and pepper. Also add 2 tins of chopped tomatoes and one and a half cups of sweetcorn. Combine the ingredients and cook until the mixture has slightly thickened and reduced.

Step 4: Construct the Pie
Line a dish with corn tortillas. (You can swap for wheat tortillas if you prefer.) In the picture, I have used a four portion pie dish but the recipe makes up to 8 portions. Add an inch layer of the meat and veg mixture and top it with tortilla. Repeat this process until you have completed three layers. Cover your final layer with tortilla leaving a couple of inches for the topping.

Step 5: Top the Pie
To complete the pie add a thick layer of Greek yoghurt. Level it out and sprinkle with mature grated cheddar. Bake the pie in the oven at 200° for around an hour until the cheese topping is golden brown.

Step 6: Serve and Scoff!
I know it is hard, but try to leave the pie to cool for a while as it helps the topping to set before you cut it.I hope you enjoy this recipe, it is the first time I have ever shared it. MAKE, BAKE & PARTAKE! 😊
