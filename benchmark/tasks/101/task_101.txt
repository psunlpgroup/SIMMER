Task: How to make custard yogurt toast

--- Plan ---

Step 1: Prepare the Custard Mixture
In a small bowl, whisk together egg and yogurt; it's 2 Tablespoon per egg which should be enough for 2 breads. Combine well and the mixture should look like custard without any egg clumps.

Step 2: Prepare the Bread
Using the back of a spoon make some dents on the bread leaving the sides.Now pour in the simple custard mixture into bread slices.Top with fruits of your choice. Berries pair really well.

Step 3: 
Bake in the oven for just 10 minutes at 400 F 200 C until custard settled and bread gets toasted.Drizzle some honey or maple syrup and serve warm. Yogurt Toast Recipe Variations:Dairy Free: The only dairy used in the recipe is Yogurt which you can substitute with plant based one.Play With Fruits: It's great to use summer berries when they are in the season but the recipe is not limited to them only.Kids Special: Kids love their food bit fun and they are over the moon when it's bright and colorful.Use Other Healthy Ingredients : Make it even healthy by adding some nut butter.If you like easy breakfast recipe, you might like my other recipes too.
