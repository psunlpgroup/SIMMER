Task: How to Make Plain Dragon Fruit Jam

--- Plan ---

Step 1: Assemble all of your ingredients.
Cut the dragon fruit in half and scoop out the pulp, squeeze out the juice from the lemon, measure your sugar and butter etc.

Step 2: Pour in the juice, crack the eggs, add the sugar, butter and dragon fruit pulp in a mixing bowl and whisk until all the ingredients are combined together.
.

Step 3: Pour the whisked mixture into a double boiler.
.

Step 4: Cook over a low and steady heat.
Stir frequently. The mixture will begin to thicken as you stir, over a period of about half an hour.  Add some sugar if it is not sweet enough, or water if it's too sweet. Check to see if it's thick enough.

Step 5: Pour the jam in a container or jar and seal.
Finished - spread your jam on a bread etc.
