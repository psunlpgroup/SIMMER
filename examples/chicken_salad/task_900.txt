Task: How to make a chicken salad

--- Plan ---

Step 1: Slice the chicken
Slice the raw chicken breast on a cutting board.

Step 2: Fry the chicken
Heat oil in a pan on the stove and fry the chicken until cooked through.

Step 3: Chop the vegetables
Chop the lettuce on a cutting board.

Step 4: Assemble the salad
Combine the fried chicken and the chopped lettuce in a bowl.

Step 5: Serve
Serve the finished salad on a plate.
